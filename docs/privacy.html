Privacy Policy

Brier Score Trainer · Last updated: October 21, 2026

Brier Score Trainer (“the app”) is a free, personal project that lets people log probability forecasts and track how well calibrated they are. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information the app collects

  • Your Google account email address. You sign in with Google, and the app receives your email address so it can tell users apart. The app never receives your Google password.
  • The forecasts you enter: the question text, your probability, the date, the outcome you record, and the resulting score.
  • An optional display name, if you choose to join the leaderboard.

How the information is used

  • To sign you in and show you your own forecasts and statistics.
  • To calculate your scores.
  • If you join the leaderboard, other users can see your display name, your average Brier score, and your number of resolved forecasts. Your email address and your individual forecasts are never shown to other users.

Where the information is stored

The app is hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud, sign-in is handled by Google, and forecast data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase. These providers process data on the app’s behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing

Your information is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except the service providers above, or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. You can remove yourself from the leaderboard at any time inside the app. To have your account data deleted entirely, email the contact address below from the Google account you used to sign in.

Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect your data, including keeping database credentials private and limiting each user to their own records. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and children under 13 should not use it.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
