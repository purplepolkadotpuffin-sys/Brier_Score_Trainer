Brier Score Trainer

A free tool for training judgment under uncertainty. Log a probability on any yes/no question, resolve it once the outcome is known, and see how well calibrated you are using the Brier score (lower is better; 0.25 is the score of a coin flip).

Sign in with Google to keep your own private forecast log, and optionally join a leaderboard under a display name.
